Curriculum & Learning Pathways

From foundational inquiry to college-level Advanced Placement (AP) honors.

Foundational STEM

Interactive math games, hands-on science experiments, and introductory robotics build early inquiry habits.

Language Arts & Literacy

Phonics, creative writing workshops, and guided reading groups cultivate a lifelong love of literature.

Creative Arts & Expression

Visual arts, choral music, and drama foster self-expression, confidence, and motor skill development.

Integrated Life & Physical Sciences

Lab-based investigations exploring physics concepts, environmental science, and biological ecosystems.

Pre-Algebra & Algebra Foundations

Developing logical reasoning, algebraic thinking, data analysis, and geometric spatial problem solving.

World Cultures & Social Studies

Exploring global civilizations, geography, civics, and critical analysis of historical events.

Computer Science & AI Ethics

Python programming, web development, data structures, and ethical implications of artificial intelligence.

AP Biology & AP Chemistry

Rigorous college-level laboratory science courses preparing students for top university STEM majors.

AP Literature & Modern History

Advanced analytical essay writing, philosophical discourse, and thorough research methodologies.
